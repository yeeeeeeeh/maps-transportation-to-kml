Maps list → JSON

Turn a shared Google Maps list into a Google Takeout style “Saved Places.json”. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Add the bookmarklet (once)

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. If dragging is not possible, copy it and paste it as the URL of a new bookmark.

Maps list → JSON

2. Open your list and click it

Open the list link (google.com/maps/placelists/list/… or a maps.app.goo.gl link that opens it) in Google Maps, then click the bookmark. This page opens with the list converted.
